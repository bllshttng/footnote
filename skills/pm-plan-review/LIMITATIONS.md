# Pm plan review limitations

## Known Limitations and Deferred Work

- The lenses are advisory. Nothing blocks a plan dispatch on a fail, and calibration against operator labels has not run.
- A reader is one spawned session that sees only its own prompt. It cannot open the journal, another reader's output, or the repo.
- Reader cost is untracked: the wall clock lands in the judge row, but no spend meter reads it.
- The pack is present in every install with no opt-out.
- The question lenses grade the plan text alone. Only the four source lenses read an outside source.
