# partner_challenge

Fail when the plan removes or rewrites something without saying what it was for, calls a change small while the work reaches past what it names, or answers "do it right this time" with a rewrite. Pass when the plan says what the thing it touches was for, bounds the work honestly, and names what would have to be true for the design to be wrong.
