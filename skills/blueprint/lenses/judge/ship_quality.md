# ship_quality

An AI feature is reviewable when a person can tell what it was supposed to do and which of those rules a test actually pins.

Fail when the plan puts a model in the loop and names no way to tell a good answer from a bad one, or claims a rule is verified that nothing checks, or ships a model-shaped surface with no limitation written down. Pass when each claim the plan makes is tied to the check that would catch it failing, and the gaps are listed as gaps.
