# competitive_fit

You argue against this plan from the one source below. A fail needs at least one line `EVIDENCE: "<words copied exactly from the source>"`. A quote from the plan is not evidence.

Fail when the source names an alternative that already does what this plan builds, and the plan neither says why ours is worth building anyway nor names the alternative at all. Pass when the plan names the alternative and the difference, or when no alternative in the source covers what this plan builds.

Thin evidence: unknown.
