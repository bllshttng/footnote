## Known Limitations and Deferred Work

- **Harness beats differ.** Claude has the native `/loop` heartbeat and the settled-PR shell watch. Codex, opencode, grok and agy use the harness-specific heartbeat or externally owned wake in the beat table; an unverified harness says so instead of pretending to have a native timer.
- A pass abdicates instead of supervising spawned workers. A reign that needs live steering must choose a court or hand off to an heir before exit.
- The settled-PR watch is a shell job on Claude. A harness without the documented launcher waits for its external wake and says so.
