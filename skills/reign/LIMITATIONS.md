# Limitations

- **Codex has no beat.** Codex exposes none of `/goal`, `/loop`, or Monitor, so a codex reign's only beat is the wake arm's backstop. The skill says so in its first line and a codex reign must plan around it: the check-in body runs on wakes (mail, operator turns), never on a schedule.
- **`--once` defers to king-for-a-day** until the one-wave fold lands. The pass posture is not re-implemented here; `--once` prints the pointer and stops.
- **Court's crown-source field may be absent.** Until the crown-durability change lands in `fno agents court`, the crown check falls back to `reign_state(scope)` and prints `CROWN-SOURCE: reign_state (court field absent)`. The refusal on a split works either way.
- **The six monitors are claude-shaped.** Harness-tracked Monitor with a shell until-loop is the reference arming; a harness without an equivalent arms what it has and says so, rather than silently running a beatless reign.
