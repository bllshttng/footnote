## Known Limitations and Deferred Work

- **Codex has no beat.** Codex exposes none of `/goal`, `/loop`, or Monitor. A codex reign's only beat is the wake arm's backstop. The skill says so in its first line. A codex reign plans around it: the check-in body runs on wakes, never on a schedule.
- **A pass abdicates instead of supervising spawned workers.** A reign that needs live steering must choose a court or hand off to an heir before exit.
- **The one monitor is claude-shaped.** A harness-tracked Monitor with a shell until-loop is the reference arming. A harness without one arms what it has and says so. It never runs a silent, beatless reign.
