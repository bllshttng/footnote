# Brand identity

The brand identity growth-studio drafts under: the brand name, the founder,
and the positioning a draft speaks from.
This file is the worked example and fallback bundled with the pack; an
installing project points `[context.artifacts] brand-identity` at its own
identity so a second project never drafts under Footnote's name.
The role agents read this before drafting. brand-check enforces the voice
contract and the founder-name forms declared under `## Founder`; other identity
adherence (brand positioning, third-person voice) is a drafting discipline the
agents carry, not an automated gate.

## Founder

The founder-name forms brand-check validates. A draft carrying a `never` form
fails brand review; the `formal` and `byline` forms pass. The `byline`
argument to `/fno:growth-launch` emits the `byline` form on founder-voice
surfaces.
