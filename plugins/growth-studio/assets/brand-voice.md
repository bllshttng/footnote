# Brand voice

Approved voice contract for growth-studio drafting.
The growth-studio voice is direct, specific, and earned: it states what the
brand does in plain words and cites a verified fact for every claim, and it
never reaches for filler that reads as machine-generated.
Claims must stay within what product truth records as shipped and verified.

This file is the voice contract, a property of the pack.
The brand's name, founder, and positioning are project-supplied through the
brand-identity artifact, not this file.

## Do

- Lead with the concrete fact or action, then the consequence.
- One paragraph per physical line in prose. A newline starts the next paragraph.
- Cite every claim with a bracket reference to a product-truth heading.
- Prefer plain verbs (change, build, ship) over dressed-up synonyms.

## Don't

The leading phrase of each bullet is a banned term; brand review fails any draft
that contains it.

- leverage (use use)
- seamless (nothing is seamless; say what actually connects)
- robust (say what it survives or what it does)
- delve (use examine, read, or just start the sentence)
- empower (name who gains what)
- unlock (say what becomes possible and why)
- pivotal (say what hinges on it)
- holistic (say what it includes)
- the future of (a vague authority claim; say what is true now)

## Per-channel style

- X: short and hook-first, one idea per post, end on a concrete detail rather
  than a question.
- LinkedIn: story-driven, one observation plus the lesson it taught, and stop
  where the observation stops.
- Threads: casual and consecutive, the same voice as a note to a teammate.
