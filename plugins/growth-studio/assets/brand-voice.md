# Brand voice

Approved brand context for growth-studio drafting.

Claims must stay within what product truth records as shipped and verified.
A packaged role that drafts claims is a role that can invent them, which is why
factual review and brand review are required evaluators, not optional ones.

This asset is referenced as a ContextKind.ARTIFACT context reference. Naming the
reference does not grant access to the content; the read boundary still enforces
access at resolution time.
